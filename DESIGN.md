# Sweet Main design

Sweet Main is a directory of independent local businesses. Neighbors use it to
find a shop near them; owners (often not very techy) use it to list their
business for free. The feeling is warm and neighborly, plain like Nextdoor,
never "AI template".

## The idea

The site borrows from a real Main Street: the green street-name sign, and the
painted green-and-gold storefronts under it. That is the one bold thing.
Everything else stays quiet: white page, dark ink text, green for actions.

## Color

| Name          | Hex       | Use                                              |
|---------------|-----------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Sign green    | `#0B6B3A` | Logo sign, buttons, links, active states, footer |
| Sign green dk | `#08552E` | Button hover                                     |
| Gilt          | `#F2B233` | Small highlights only: "Raising funds" badges, awning stripes |
| Ink           | `#1E2420` | Text, line drawings                              |
| Muted         | `#5F6B64` | Secondary text                                   |
| Sidewalk      | `#F3F4F1` | Quiet surfaces, footer strip of the hero         |
| Line          | `#E2E5E0` | Borders, dividers                                |

Light theme only. No gradients, no drop shadows except the search box.

## Type

One family: **Overpass**, an open-source take on the US highway/street-sign
lettering. Bold (700–800) for the logo and headings, 400 for body text.
Sentence case everywhere. No all-caps labels (the only capitals are the shop
signs in the street drawing, because real shop signs look like that).

Scale: 15 / 17 (body) / 20 / 26 / 34 / 52.

## Shape

- Radius 6px on buttons, inputs and the logo sign; 10px on photos.
- Business cards are photo + text, no box, no border, no shadow.

## Writing

Plain and specific. Say what things are ("Bakery in Springfield, OR"), not
slogans. Join details with words, not middle dots. No arrows on link text.
Buttons say exactly what they do ("Publish listing", "Get directions").

## Don't

- Cream background with a serif and a terracotta accent.
- Identical rounded cards with grey shadows.
- Emoji as icons, numbered 01/02/03 markers, eyebrow labels above headings.
- Animating every section on scroll.
